## Design Context

### Users
Algerian e-commerce merchants (store owners/managers, typically small-to-medium businesses). They work on CommerceCraft during business hours, often on laptops in shops/offices, managing products, AI landing pages, orders, shipping (ZR Express, Yalidin, NOEST), and multiple stores. Mostly non-technical — the interface must feel trustworthy, calm, and effortless.

### Brand Personality
Crafted · Confident · Warm
CommerceCraft = commerce + craft: the tool helps merchants "craft" their storefronts. The interface should feel handmade with care, precise in execution, and warm enough to feel like a helpful business partner rather than cold software. Emotional goal: the merchant feels in control and proud of what they are building.

### Aesthetic Direction
"Artisanal editorial" — a warm, print-inspired admin. Think a beautifully typeset small-batch catalog or a craftsman's ledger: warm cream paper surfaces, espresso ink, and a single confident rust/vermilion accent. Editorial typography carries the design (generous modular scale, tight tracking on display type, refined hierarchy). NOT: generic indigo-on-gray shadcn dashboards, glassmorphism, gradient text, neon dark modes, the "hero metric template" cliché, or identical repeating card grids.

### Design Principles
1. Craft over chrome — typography, spacing, and proportion carry the design; decorative UI is minimal and purposeful.
2. Warmth in every surface — cream/warm-tinted neutrals (never pure #fff/#000), tinted toward the brand hue.
3. One confident accent — rust/vermilion used sparingly (60-30-10); its rarity is its power.
4. Editorial hierarchy — display type (Bricolage Grotesque) + refined body (Schibsted Grotesk); generous asymmetric whitespace.
5. Calm motion — subtle expo-eased entrances, transform/opacity only.

### Theme
Light (warm). Merchants use the platform during business hours.
